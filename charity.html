Our Charity Commitment

A promise to use the power of music and events to support people, communities and the world around us.

50%

Of Event Profits for Charity

DJ Sniper India is committed to donating 50% of the profit earned from every event to charitable and social welfare initiatives across our focus areas.

The commitment applies to event profit, not total ticket sales or gross revenue. Donation details will be published when verified records are available.

Why We Do It

Music creates connection. Events create opportunity. We want that energy to reach beyond the stage—towards education, health, inclusion, culture, animal care and a healthier planet.

We aim to work with credible community groups and service organisations, and to share updates on initiatives as they are completed.

Transparency & Accountability

Trust matters. We will distinguish our commitment from completed donations and publish only details that can be supported by records.

01 — Track Event Profit

Record event income and eligible expenses so the profit calculation can be explained.

02 — Direct Support

Allocate the committed share to selected charitable initiatives or suitable partner organisations.

03 — Share Verified Updates

Publish available donation confirmations, activity summaries and impact updates after verification.

No donation amount or completed activity is claimed on this page unless and until it is documented and verified.
